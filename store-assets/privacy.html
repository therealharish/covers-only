Privacy without a playlist of caveats.

Effective September 24, 2026

Covers Only processes YouTube Mix metadata locally. Preferences and a bounded recent-video history can sync through Chrome Sync; the developer never receives, sells, or shares this data.

Data processed

On YouTube, the extension reads the current page URL, video ID, title, channel name, visible description, and visible Mix entries. It uses these details only to classify likely musical covers and control playback.

Data stored and synchronized

  • The enabled toggle and playback preferences.
  • Custom include and exclude keywords.
  • Video IDs explicitly marked always allow or always block.
  • Up to 120 recently played video IDs and playback timestamps, used to reduce repetition.

This data uses Chrome extension sync storage, so Chrome can synchronize it through the Google account connected to the browser when Chrome Sync is enabled. It is never sent to the developer or a developer-operated service.

No collection or sharing

There is no analytics, advertising, account system, external API, or developer-operated server. Other than Chrome's built-in synchronization, the extension makes no external requests and does not access cookies, credentials, general browsing history, private playlists, comments, or Google Account data.

Permissions

Storage synchronizes preferences, recent video IDs/timestamps, and video decisions. YouTube site access reads visible Mix metadata and operates YouTube's native Next and Pause controls for the filtering feature.
